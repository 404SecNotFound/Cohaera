# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best for the project as a whole

Unacceptable behaviour:

* Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## A note specific to this project

Cohaera is a security project, and security work involves telling people their
work is wrong. **Say the finding, not the person.** "This check can be defeated
by padding the session" is the job. "Whoever wrote this does not understand
detection" is not, and it is also less useful — a finding that names a mechanism
can be fixed.

Adversarial contributions are welcome and encouraged: new evasions, defect
reproductions, and evidence that a published number is wrong are among the most
valuable things anyone can send. Adversarial *conduct* is a different thing.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through **GitHub private vulnerability reporting**
on this repository (*Security → Report a vulnerability*), which reaches the
maintainers privately. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.

Maintainers will use these guidelines in determining consequences:

1. **Correction** — a private written warning, with clarity about the nature of
   the violation and why it was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including
   no interaction with those involved for a specified period.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
   communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any sort of public interaction
   within the community.
